Privacy Policy — video-uploader

Last updated: 23 October 2026

video-uploader is a personal tool used only by its developer, Kanta Hasada, to manage the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

Through the YouTube API Services, the app accesses the developer's own YouTube channel information, video metadata (titles, descriptions, captions, schedules) and channel statistics, in order to upload and manage the developer's own videos.

How data is stored and shared

  • Authorization tokens and data are stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data from other users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/kanta0410/privacy.
